# 1. Vision & Problem Statement

Kisekka Market is Kampala's largest and most established spare parts hub — an informal, community-driven economy with its own social hierarchy, trust systems, and communication culture. Despite the volume of daily trade, critical inefficiencies cost traders real revenue every day.

### Core Pain Points

- Traders lose customers when they can't find a part — allies exhaust their stock and the sale dies
- Finding a part requires physically sending an employee across the market, wasting time and labour
- Community announcements (weddings, burials, meetings) depend on physical MCs — limited reach
- Dealers post inventory on WhatsApp Status but reach is limited to personal contacts only
- No public channel exists that respects the market's social dynamics (pricing privacy, supplier secrecy)

### Vision Statement

Kisekka Online is a community-first mobile app that connects every trader, dealer, and member of Kisekka Market — enabling faster part discovery, wider announcement reach, and a digital presence layer that respects how the market actually works.

# 2. Market Intelligence

These insights come from direct observation and conversations inside Kisekka Market. They shape every product decision.

## Social & Business Dynamics

- Traders WILL NOT publicly show out-of-stock status — it signals weakness
- Pricing is never disclosed publicly — it destroys negotiation leverage
- Supplier networks are guarded — middleman culture is normal and protected
- A junior trader will not casually engage a senior dealer — hierarchy is real
- Bragging and status signalling is a genuine motivation — big dealers want to project success
- Shop A/B daily credit balancing is common — an informal trust-based credit system

# 4. Product Strategy

## Core Design Principles

- **LEADS GENERATOR** — not a transaction platform. Conversations move to WhatsApp/calls as normal
- Follow model over group model — respects social hierarchy, no forced interaction
- Demand-side posting — 'I'm looking for X' is lower friction than sellers updating stock
- Private responses — replies go to DM/WhatsApp, not public feed
- Clean & modern UI — users already use social media, design to match that familiarity

## What We Are NOT Building (Intentionally)

- No in-app chat (WhatsApp already owns this)
- No public pricing display
- No forced inventory management
- No transaction/payment layer (not yet)

# 7. Go-To-Market Strategy

## Launch Strategy

- Use existing market MCs to announce the app — trusted voice, familiar channel, brilliant distribution fit
- Leverage personal relationships inside the market for early beta users
- Seed the feed with content before launch — avoid empty app problem
- Target 5 high-profile dealers early — their presence signals credibility to smaller traders

## Early Validation Signals

- 5 traders already expressed interest after initial conversations
- WhatsApp Status behaviour confirms digital content motivation already exists
- MC announcement model confirms willingness to pay for reach
